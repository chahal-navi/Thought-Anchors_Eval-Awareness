Counterfactual importance of each sentence, per prompt

For every sentence boundary i we keep the first i sentences of the chain of thought, let the model write the rest many times (50 rollouts for Qwen, 20–40 for Gemma), and record the share of continuations that conclude EVALUATION. A sentence’s importance is how much that share changes when the sentence is kept rather than replaced by something else the model might have written.

Importancei = Pi+1 − Pi, where Pi is the share of resampled continuations ending in EVALUATION when the first i sentences are kept. A continuation counts at position i only if its first new sentence differs from the original (cosine similarity < 0.8). With 50 rollouts the standard error of one importance value is about 0.07–0.10, so effects under ~0.2 are mostly noise. Lock-in = the first i after which P stays within 0.15 of its final value.
